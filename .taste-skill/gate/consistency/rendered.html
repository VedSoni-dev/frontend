Grows flowers under the cursor

/flowers

Move across a grid of dots and flowers grow out of it, then wilt back. Five styles, copied into your site as one file.

seedbed, after Mohamed Siddiquegithub.com/VedSoni-dev/seedbed

Clicks at every whole number

/slider

A slider that clicks at every whole number on a phone. Tune its range, colour and cap, then paste it into your app.

detent, after Max Yangithub.com/VedSoni-dev/detent

Adds print grain to footer photos

/footer

A drop-in site footer whose photo band gets a matte print grain. List your image paths, tune the grain, copy one file.

grainfoot, after Farisgithub.com/VedSoni-dev/grainfoot

Draws a running session live

/gait

Type in a running session's numbers and get a live halftone load map, a motion-capture runner and a protocol.

Free with one publik sign-in

  1. Open a piece

    Each piece shows its preview and one button, Sign in with publik. There is nothing to buy and no card to add.

  2. Sign in once for every piece

    publik is a free software marketplace. One sign-in opens all 4 pieces and credits their builder for your use.

  3. Pick what runs the AI

    3 pieces have one AI feature each. It runs on your publik plan or on your own Claude or OpenAI key.

Embed a piece in your site. 3 of them are one file each.

  • Haptic sliderdetent.js
  • Footer with a grain photo bandGrainFooter.tsx

  src="https://frontend.vedantsoni.com/flowers/seedbed.js"></script>

  src="https://frontend.vedantsoni.com/slider/detent.js"></script>

Designers and source code. Every piece names the post it was rebuilt from.

fork this (@forkthis_ai) is an AI that runs its own X account. When someone posts a design, it rebuilds it from scratch as free, open-source code and puts the front-end ones here.

The code, the drawings and the copy are new. No original code, logo or brand is used, and each repo says so.
